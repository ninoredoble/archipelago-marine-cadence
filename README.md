# Archipelago Marine Cadence

> **Philippine Coral Reef Telemetry & Oceanographic Sanctuary Explorer**  
> An interactive marine cartography and telemetry platform mapping key marine protected areas across the Coral Triangle—from Macajalar Bay (Cagayan de Oro) to Tubbataha Reefs and the Philippine Trench.

---

## 🌊 Overview

The Philippines anchors the epicenter of global marine shorefish and coral diversity (The Coral Triangle). 

**Archipelago Marine Cadence** models oceanographic telemetry across 8 critical marine protected areas (MPAs), tracing bathymetric depth gradients, pelagic current highways, and benthic habitat health. It provides researchers and ocean enthusiasts with an interactive simulation interface to inspect sea surface temperature (SST) anomalies and predict coral bleaching vulnerabilities.

---

## 🧭 Featured Marine Sanctuaries

1. **Macajalar Bay Marine Sanctuary (Cagayan de Oro / Misamis Oriental)**:
   - High benthic biodiversity directly influenced by the Cagayan River watershed. Upstream siltation mitigation measures have restored underwater visibility, supporting resilient *Acropora* staghorn coral recolonization.
2. **Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park (UNESCO / Sulu Sea)**:
   - Apex coral atoll wilderness hosting 360+ coral species and apex whitetip reef sharks.
3. **Apo Reef Natural Park (Occidental Mindoro)**:
   - Largest contiguous coral atoll system in the country with dramatic deep-wall drop-offs.
4. **Camiguin Black Coral Sanctuary (Bohol Sea)**:
   - Rare volcanic benthic shelves harboring deep-water black coral (*Antipathes*) and giant clams (*Tridacna*).
5. **Tañon Strait Protected Seascape (Cebu-Negros Channel)**:
   - Major cetacean migration highway for spinner dolphins and dwarf sperm whales.
6. **Monad Shoal Pelagic Sanctuary (Malapascua, Cebu)**:
   - The planet's premier daily oceanic cleaning station for pelagic thresher sharks.
7. **Coron Island Biosphere Reserve (Calamian Islands, Palawan)**:
   - Ancestral waters managed by the Tagbanwa people with karst lagoons and dugong seagrass beds.
8. **Siargao Pelagic Shelf (Surigao del Norte)**:
   - Deep oceanic drop-off directly fronting the 10,540-meter-deep Philippine Trench.

## ⚡ Technical Capabilities

- **Interactive Cartographic Canvas**: Vector-rendered Philippine archipelago with bathymetric graticules, trench abyss lines, and dynamic current flow animations.
- **Dossier Biosignature System**: Dino-Draft inspired taxon/sanctuary carousel providing immediate bio-index health scoring, benthic depth profiles, and keystone species catalogs.
- **Benthic Thermal Anomaly Simulator**: Interactive slider simulating +0.0°C to +3.0°C SST departures to calculate real-time bleaching probability and thermal stress alerts.
- **Zero-Dependency Architecture**: Built exclusively with Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript, responsive SVG viewport matrices, and customized dark oceanic CSS tokens.
